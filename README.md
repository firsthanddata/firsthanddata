### Firsthand Data

Fresh job data straight from company career pages on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters:
official public job board APIs, only the new jobs, salaries as numbers.

**Job data robots on Apify**

| Robot | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Job Monitor](https://apify.com/firsthand-data/career-page-job-monitor) | New jobs from the companies you care about, every morning |
| [Career Page Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/firsthand-data/career-page-jobs-scraper) | Every open job of any company on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or SmartRecruiters |
| [Remote Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/firsthand-data/remote-jobs-scraper) | Remote jobs from 1,040 remote-first companies |
| [Startup Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/firsthand-data/startup-jobs-scraper) | Jobs from 2,344 startups that hire through Ashby |

**Hiring Pulse**: what 60,000+ live job posts say about remote work and pay, every two weeks.
Issues and data: [hiring-pulse](https://github.com/firsthanddata/hiring-pulse).
